SOURCES & LIMITATIONS

What these values mean

  1. State and locality results are matched with Open-Meteo geocoding.
  2. Air quality uses Open-Meteo's CAMS global atmospheric composition model; weather uses its forecast models.
  3. The shown AQI is the model provider's US AQI, not the Indian CPCB scale.

Open-Meteo regional model resolution is approximately 45 km for air quality, so it cannot resolve a district boundary or construction-site conditions. AQI depends on pollutant averaging and model uncertainty. Use official local advisories and onsite instruments for decisions.
